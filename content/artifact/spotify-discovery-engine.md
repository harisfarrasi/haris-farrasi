Streaming can look like a catalog business. If every platform has millions of songs, then the real product moat shifts from access to **discovery**.

Spotify's product advantage is that it shortens the distance between a vague mood and a satisfying next listen. The user does not open Spotify because they lack music. They open it because they want help choosing.

That is why playlists, daily mixes, radio, editorial curation, and recommendation models all matter together. Discovery is not one feature. It is the operating system of retention.

## Product Lesson

When supply is abundant, value moves to navigation. Products win not only by having more inventory, but by helping users reach the right inventory with less effort.
