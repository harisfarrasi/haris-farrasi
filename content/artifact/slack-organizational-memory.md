Chat products usually get framed as speed tools. Slack's stronger insight is that conversation becomes valuable when it stays recoverable.

Inside a growing company, decisions are constantly scattered across channels, threads, and direct messages. The PM challenge is not only sending updates. It is preserving enough context that the next person can move without re-asking the same questions.

Slack works when it balances two jobs at once:

1. Fast coordination in the moment.
2. Useful retrieval weeks later.

That changes how we should evaluate the product. Search quality, thread structure, channel naming, and app integrations are not secondary. They decide whether the tool becomes noise or memory.

## Product Lesson

Internal tools become strategic when they reduce the cost of context recovery. A company that can refind its own decisions moves faster than one that keeps re-explaining itself.

## What To Study

1. Search relevance across messy workplace language.
2. Channel structure as information architecture.
3. Notifications versus long-term information quality.
4. Integrations that turn chat into a system of record.
